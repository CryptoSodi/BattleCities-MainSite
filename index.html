Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you ape in — read this before you buy $NEKO.

What exactly is NEKOPIXEL ($NEKO)?
NEKOPIXEL is a community-driven BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain, built around a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply, locked liquidity, and renounced contract ownership. Pixel-art branding, backed by on-chain mechanics anyone can verify.
How does the presale work?
The presale runs in fixed-price stages. Stage 1 already sold out at $0.0012, Stage 2 is live now at $0.0018, and price steps up again at Stage 3. Once a stage sells out — or the countdown timer hits zero — the price moves to the next stage and doesn't come back down.
Is the contract and liquidity actually locked?
Yes. The liquidity pool contract is locked for 24 months and ownership has been renounced, so no one — including the team — can mint new $NEKO or alter core contract parameters after launch. Always verify the lock and audit yourself before buying.
What happens after the presale ends?
Once the presale target is reached, liquidity is added and $NEKO lists on DEX at the listing price. Presale tokens are sent directly to the wallet address used to buy — the same one connected in the "Buy $NEKO" section above.
Is $NEKO risky? Should I be worried?
Yes — like any early-stage crypto asset, $NEKO is highly volatile and comes with no guaranteed returns. This is a high-risk, high-reward zone. Only commit funds you can afford to lose, and always DYOR before buying.
