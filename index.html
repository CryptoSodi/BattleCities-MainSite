Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you deploy — read this before you buy $BATC.

What exactly is $BATC?
$BATC is the planned BattleCities SPL token on Solana with a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply. The mint address will be published when the token launches—ignore any address shared before that announcement.
How does the presale work?
The presale runs in fixed-price stages. A stage changes only when its BATC allocation sells out. The entire presale closes when the countdown reaches zero, even if the current stage still has tokens available.
Is the contract and liquidity actually locked?
The BATC mint and liquidity are not live yet. Their addresses, authority settings, and lock details will be published for independent on-chain verification before launch.
What happens after the presale ends?
On devnet, the API verifies the confirmed SOL transfer, records the purchase, creates your Token-2022 account when needed, and delivers test BATC automatically. Payment and BATC delivery each have their own explorer transaction.
Is $BATC risky? Should I be worried?
Yes — like any early-stage crypto asset, $BATC is highly volatile and comes with no guaranteed returns. Only commit funds you can afford to lose, and always DYOR before buying.
